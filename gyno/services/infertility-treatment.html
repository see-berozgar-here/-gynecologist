Struggling to conceive can be an emotionally exhausting journey. At Aarohi Women's Clinic, Dr. Ananya Sharma offers a thorough, empathetic fertility evaluation and a personalised treatment roadmap to help couples achieve their dream of parenthood.

Understanding Infertility

Infertility is defined as the inability to conceive after 12 months of regular, unprotected intercourse (6 months if the woman is above 35). It affects approximately 1 in 6 couples globally, and in India, an estimated 15–20% of couples experience difficulty conceiving at some point in their reproductive years.

Infertility can have causes in either the woman, the man, or both partners. A thorough evaluation is essential before initiating treatment. At Aarohi Clinic, we take a systematic, evidence-based approach to identify the root cause and recommend the most effective treatment pathway.

Common Causes of Female Infertility

  • Ovulation disorders — PCOS, hypothalamic dysfunction, premature ovarian failure
  • Fallopian tube blockage due to pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) or previous infections
  • Endometriosis — uterine lining growing outside the uterus
  • Uterine fibroids or polyps affecting implantation
  • Cervical mucus hostility impeding sperm passage
  • Thyroid disorders — hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism affecting fertility
  • Elevated prolactin levels suppressing ovulation
  • Unexplained infertility — no identifiable cause after full evaluation

Fertility Evaluation at Aarohi Clinic

We begin with a detailed medical history and a structured fertility workup for both partners:

  • Hormonal panel: FSH, LH, AMH (ovarian reserve), estradiol, prolactin, thyroid function, testosterone
  • Pelvic ultrasound: Assessment of ovaries, uterus, follicle count (antral follicle count), and fibroid or cyst detection
  • Hysterosalpingography (HSG): X-ray dye test to assess fallopian tube patency
  • Semen analysis: Evaluation of sperm count, motility, morphology, and volume
  • Follicular monitoring: Serial ultrasound to track ovulation timing

Treatment Options We Offer

  • Ovulation Induction (OI): Medication (Clomiphene, Letrozole) to stimulate egg release in women with ovulation disorders
  • Timed Intercourse Programme: Ultrasound-guided follicular monitoring to identify the exact fertile window
  • Intrauterine Insemination (IUI): Processed sperm directly placed in the uterus around ovulation — a minimally invasive fertility treatment
  • Hormonal Therapy: Correction of thyroid, prolactin, or LH/FSH imbalances affecting fertility
  • Lifestyle Optimisation: Dietary guidance, weight management, and stress reduction to naturally boost fertility
  • Referral to IVF Centre: For cases requiring In-Vitro Fertilisation or advanced reproductive technology, we provide expert referrals with full case documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should we try before seeing a fertility specialist?
+
If you are under 35, try naturally for 12 months before seeking evaluation. If you are 35 or older, seek help after 6 months. If you have known conditions like PCOS, irregular periods, endometriosis, or previous pelvic infections, visit a specialist immediately without waiting.
What is IUI and how successful is it?
+
IUI (Intrauterine Insemination) involves placing washed, concentrated sperm directly into the uterus at the time of ovulation. Success rates per cycle are typically 10–20%, depending on age, diagnosis, and the quality of sperm. It is often tried for 3–6 cycles before progressing to IVF if needed.
Does PCOS always cause infertility?
+
No — PCOS is a common but very treatable cause of ovulatory infertility. Many women with PCOS conceive successfully with ovulation induction medication or lifestyle modification. With appropriate treatment, the pregnancy rates are very encouraging.
Is infertility treatment expensive?
+
At Aarohi Clinic, we start with the least invasive and most affordable options first. Initial evaluation and ovulation induction are significantly more affordable than IVF. We believe every couple deserves a fair shot at parenthood without financial barriers, and we guide you through the most cost-effective options step by step.
