💊 Preventive Health

Women's Health Checkup — Prevention is the Best Medicine

Many serious women's health conditions — cervical cancer, breast cancer, thyroid disorders, and diabetes — are completely preventable or curable when detected early. Our comprehensive annual wellness exam is designed to keep you ahead of health risks before they become problems.

Why Annual Women's Health Checkups Matter

Women's bodies go through significant hormonal, physiological, and metabolic changes across different life stages — adolescence, reproductive years, pregnancy, perimenopause, and beyond. Each stage brings its own set of health risks and screening requirements.

An annual gynecological wellness exam allows Dr. Sharma to track changes over time, catch early warning signs, update vaccinations, and provide counselling tailored to your current life stage and health concerns.

What's Included in Our Women's Wellness Exam

  • Pap Smear (Cervical Cytology): Screening for cervical cancer and pre-cancerous changes — recommended every 3 years for women aged 21–65, or annually for those with risk factors.
  • Breast Examination: Clinical breast exam (CBE) to detect lumps, asymmetry, or skin changes. Mammography referral if indicated (age 40+).
  • Pelvic Examination: Visual and manual assessment of vagina, cervix, uterus, and ovaries.
  • Pelvic Ultrasound: Screening for ovarian cysts, fibroids, endometrial polyps, and uterine abnormalities.
  • Thyroid Function Test (TFT): TSH, T3, T4 — thyroid disorders are extremely common in women and often go undiagnosed.
  • Hormonal Panel: FSH, LH, estradiol, prolactin, testosterone — evaluated based on symptoms and age.
  • Blood Sugar & Insulin: Fasting glucose, HbA1c — particularly important for women with PCOS or family history of diabetes.
  • Lipid Profile: Cholesterol and triglyceride assessment for cardiovascular risk evaluation.
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC): Haemoglobin, platelet count — to detect anaemia and blood disorders.
  • Bone Density Screening: DEXA scan referral for perimenopausal or postmenopausal women at risk for osteoporosis.
  • HPV Vaccination Counselling: For eligible women (9–45 years) — the most effective prevention against cervical cancer.

When Should You Book a Women's Health Checkup?

  • Every woman aged 18+ should have at least an annual gynecological consultation
  • Pap smear: First exam at age 21, then every 3 years (or sooner with risk factors)
  • Before planning a pregnancy — preconception health optimisation
  • If you have noticed any unusual symptoms — bleeding, discharge, pelvic pain
  • During perimenopause for hormone evaluation and cancer screening
  • After completing your family, for permanent contraception counselling and ongoing wellness

FAQs

Is a Pap smear painful?
+
A Pap smear is a quick, simple procedure that takes about 2–3 minutes. Most women experience only mild discomfort or pressure — it should not be painful. Dr. Sharma performs the procedure gently and respectfully, ensuring you are comfortable throughout. If you experience significant pain, please let her know immediately.
Do I need a health checkup even if I feel completely healthy?
+
Absolutely. Many serious conditions — including early cervical cancer, thyroid disease, and ovarian cysts — cause no symptoms until they reach an advanced stage. Routine screening is the only way to detect these conditions early, when treatment is most effective. Feeling healthy is a great starting point — let's help you stay that way.
Can I visit for a health checkup during my period?
+
For a Pap smear, it is best to avoid the appointment during heavy bleeding as it may affect the accuracy of the test. However, a general consultation, blood tests, and ultrasound can be performed at any time of the cycle. Please inform us when booking and we can advise you on the best timing for your visit.
