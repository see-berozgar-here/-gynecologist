Menopause Care — Embrace This New Chapter with Confidence

Menopause is a natural transition, not a disease. But its symptoms can be challenging and disruptive. At Aarohi Women's Clinic, Dr. Ananya Sharma provides expert, personalised menopause management to help every woman feel her best through this change.

Understanding Menopause

Menopause is defined as the permanent cessation of menstrual periods for 12 consecutive months, marking the end of a woman's reproductive years. It typically occurs between ages 45–55, with the average age in Indian women being around 47–50 years. The years leading up to menopause — known as perimenopause — can begin years earlier and bring significant hormonal fluctuations and symptoms.

Early menopause (before age 40) — called Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (POI) — requires special evaluation and management to protect long-term bone and cardiovascular health.

Common Menopause Symptoms We Manage

  • Hot flashes and night sweats — sudden waves of heat, often followed by chills
  • Irregular and eventually absent periods during perimenopause
  • Vaginal dryness, itching, and discomfort during intercourse
  • Sleep disturbances — insomnia and frequent night waking
  • Mood changes, irritability, anxiety, and difficulty concentrating ("brain fog")
  • Fatigue and reduced energy levels
  • Joint pain and muscle aches
  • Reduced sex drive (low libido)
  • Weight gain — particularly around the abdomen
  • Urinary urgency, frequency, and increased UTI susceptibility
  • Thinning hair and changes in skin texture

Our Menopause Management Approach

  • Evaluation: FSH, LH, estradiol, AMH, thyroid panel, lipid profile, bone density screening (DEXA scan referral), and cardiovascular risk assessment.
  • Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT): Where clinically appropriate, low-dose HRT — estrogen alone or combined estrogen-progesterone — is the most effective treatment for hot flashes, vaginal dryness, and osteoporosis prevention. We discuss benefits and risks in detail for each patient.
  • Non-hormonal Options: For women who cannot take HRT, effective non-hormonal medications (SSRIs, clonidine) and evidence-based herbal/lifestyle approaches.
  • Bone Health: Calcium, Vitamin D3 supplementation, and lifestyle advice to reduce osteoporosis risk. DEXA scan referral where indicated.
  • Cardiovascular Support: Lifestyle guidance and monitoring for blood pressure, cholesterol, and metabolic changes after menopause.
  • Vaginal Health: Local estrogen (vaginal cream or pessaries) for dryness, discomfort, and urinary symptoms — safe even for women who cannot take systemic HRT.

FAQs

Is HRT safe?
+
Modern HRT is considered safe for most healthy women under 60 who start it within 10 years of menopause. The small risks associated with older, outdated HRT formulations have been significantly reduced with modern preparations. Dr. Sharma will conduct a full health assessment and discuss the most suitable and safest option for your individual situation.
How long do menopause symptoms last?
+
Menopause symptoms typically last 4–8 years on average, but some women experience them for over 10 years. Hot flashes tend to be most intense in the first 1–2 years after the final period. With appropriate treatment, symptoms can be effectively managed throughout this period, significantly improving quality of life.
What is perimenopause and how is it different from menopause?
+
Perimenopause is the transitional phase leading up to menopause — it can begin 4–10 years before the final period. During perimenopause, estrogen levels fluctuate unpredictably, causing irregular periods and symptoms like hot flashes, mood swings, and sleep disruption. Menopause is confirmed after 12 consecutive months without a period.
