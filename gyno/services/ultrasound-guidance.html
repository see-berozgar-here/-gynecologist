Ultrasound Guidance — Clarity and Confidence at Every Stage

Our in-clinic ultrasound facility means you don't need to travel to a separate diagnostic centre. Dr. Ananya Sharma performs and interprets obstetric and gynecological scans with expert precision, giving you answers during the same visit.

Types of Ultrasound Scans We Offer

  • Dating Scan (6–10 weeks): Confirms the pregnancy, establishes gestational age, detects heartbeat, and checks for ectopic pregnancy.
  • NT Scan / First Trimester Screening (11–13 weeks): Measures nuchal translucency to screen for chromosomal abnormalities like Down Syndrome. Combined with blood tests for maximum accuracy.
  • Anomaly Scan / TIFFA (18–22 weeks): Detailed examination of all fetal organs, limbs, spine, and placenta position. The most comprehensive fetal anatomy assessment.
  • Growth Scan (28–36 weeks): Monitors baby's weight, position, and well-being as delivery approaches.
  • Doppler Ultrasound: Assesses blood flow through the umbilical cord, uterine arteries, and fetal brain — critical in high-risk pregnancies.
  • Follicular Monitoring: Series of scans to track follicle growth and predict ovulation — used in infertility treatment.
  • Pelvic Ultrasound (Gynecological): Evaluation of uterus, ovaries, fibroids, cysts, polyps, and endometrial thickness.
  • Transvaginal Ultrasound (TVS): Higher resolution internal scan for early pregnancy, ovarian cysts, and pelvic pathology.
  • Biophysical Profile (BPP): Combines ultrasound with fetal heart rate monitoring to assess fetal well-being in late pregnancy.

FAQs

Is ultrasound safe during pregnancy?
+
Yes — diagnostic ultrasound uses sound waves, not radiation, and has been used safely in pregnancy for over 50 years. There is no evidence of harm to mother or baby at the frequencies and durations used in standard obstetric practice. The benefits of monitoring fetal well-being through ultrasound far outweigh any theoretical concerns.
How many ultrasound scans are needed during pregnancy?
+
A routine low-risk pregnancy requires at minimum 3–4 scans: a dating scan, NT scan, anomaly scan, and a growth scan. High-risk pregnancies will require more frequent scans — sometimes every 2–4 weeks from the second trimester. Dr. Sharma will advise on the exact number based on your individual pregnancy needs.
Can I find out my baby's gender at Aarohi Clinic?
+
In India, determination and disclosure of fetal sex is prohibited under the Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques (PCPNDT) Act, 1994. Aarohi Clinic fully complies with this law. Ultrasound scans at our clinic are performed purely for medical purposes — fetal health assessment, growth monitoring, and anomaly detection.
