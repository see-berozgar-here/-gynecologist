Your Journey

Guided Through Every Step of Your Pregnancy

From your first scan to your baby's first breath, our team walks with you through every trimester with expertise, warmth, and care.

Step 01

🌱 First Trimester (Weeks 1–12)

The most critical phase. We confirm your pregnancy, schedule your first ultrasound, prescribe essential vitamins like folic acid, and screen for early complications. You'll receive a personalised antenatal plan tailored to your health history and lifestyle.

🌱
🌸
Step 02

🌸 Second Trimester (Weeks 13–27)

Your baby grows rapidly and most women feel their best during this phase. We perform detailed anomaly scans, monitor fetal development, check for gestational diabetes, and conduct targeted blood work to ensure optimal maternal and fetal health.

Step 03

🌺 Third Trimester (Weeks 28–40)

We intensify monitoring with fortnightly check-ups, assess baby's position, prepare for the mode of delivery, and guide you through birthing techniques and hospital bag preparation. Emergency protocols are explained clearly so you feel fully prepared.

🌺
🏥
Step 04

🏥 Delivery Planning

We create a customised birth plan that respects your preferences — whether normal delivery or a planned C-section. Our team coordinates with partner hospitals for a seamless delivery experience, ensuring both mother and baby are safe at every moment.

Step 05

💝 Postnatal Care

Our care continues after delivery. We monitor postpartum recovery, assist with breastfeeding challenges, screen for postpartum depression, and conduct scheduled follow-ups to ensure you heal completely and bond beautifully with your newborn.
